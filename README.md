# Internet Usage in Villages – Gurralavari Palli  
(Community Service Project)  

## Project Overview  
This project investigates Internet usage in the village of *Gurralavari Palli*, focusing on digital literacy, connectivity, and its impact on education, communication, and livelihood.  
We conducted door-to-door surveys, utilized Google Forms, and followed up with awareness sessions for village residents.

## Vision & Mission  
*Vision:* Empower rural communities with digital literacy for a smarter and connected India.  
*Mission:* Promote awareness of Internet usage for learning, agriculture, business, and social development.

## Project Components  
- A Google Form survey and the collected responses summarizing Internet usage, device access, connectivity, and challenges.  
- Field-work with photos/videos documenting village visits and interviews.  
- An awareness session for villagers, including guidelines and printed materials in Telugu and English.  
- An interactive 3D visualization of the village layout and solution concept.  
- Our proposed solution: *SafeNetBox* — a solar-powered, easy-to-use Internet device designed for rural areas, with features such as safe link blocking, offline content, and usage analytics.
